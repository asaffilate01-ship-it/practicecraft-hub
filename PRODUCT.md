# PracticeCraft / IQ Practice Cloud: product brief

UK accounting practices, practice owners, accountants, bookkeepers, clients and staff use this product for client onboarding, aml, jobs and deadlines, bookkeeping, payroll, vat, accounts, tax filing, document requests and firm operations.

Core visual identity: Existing src/index.css light UI: muted chalk HSL 80 13% 96%, deep evergreen text HSL 164 19% 11%, olive ring and dark mode blue accents; preserve established task/alert colours.

Important risks and design priorities: Make deadline, filing status, HMRC/Companies House response, approval ownership, audit trail and client confidentiality legible. Never label an unsent submission as filed, and require explicit confirmation of irreversible financial actions.

This document guides design agents; current approved functionality, permissions, laws and production integrations are authoritative. Do not invent live availability, pricing, compliance grades or customer results.
